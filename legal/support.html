Vitto Support

Help with the app, your pet and your subscription.

Need a hand? Email getvitto@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days. Telling us your device and what you were doing helps us sort it out faster.

Common questions

My steps look wrong

Vitto reads steps from Apple Health. Make sure Vitto is allowed to read Steps in the Health app (Profile → Sharing → Apps → Vitto), then tap "Steps look off? Sync now" on the Today screen.

I didn't get my sign-up code

Check your spam or junk folder. If you just asked for one, wait a minute before asking again.

How do I cancel or manage Vitto Plus?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Vitto, or use Settings → Vitto Plus in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewing. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Plus but it isn't unlocked

Open the Vitto Plus screen in the app and tap Restore, signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Can I delete what my pet remembers about me?

Yes. In Settings you can see the notes your pet keeps and delete any or all of them. Chat messages are deleted automatically after 90 days.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, pet and logs. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll do it for you.
