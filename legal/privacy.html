Vitto Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Vitto is a health app with a virtual pet: the healthy things you do help your pet grow. This policy explains what information Vitto collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. "Vitto", "we" and "us" mean Kyle Li, who runs the app.

The short version

  • We don't sell your information, and we don't show ads.
  • There are no analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • Meal photos are analysed and then thrown away. We never store them.
  • Apple Health data is only used to run Vitto. It's never used for advertising and never sold.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our login provider), your username and display name.
  • Profile and goals: the answers you choose to give during setup, such as age range, sex, height, weight, fitness goal, activity level and training preferences. These tailor your pet and your targets.
  • What you log: meals and their nutrition, workouts and lifts, steps, sleep, screen time, and mind-game results.
  • Your pet: its name, animal, personality and progress.
  • Chats with your pet: the messages you send it and its replies. On Vitto Plus, your pet also keeps short notes about things you've told it, so it can remember them later.
  • Friends and shared pets: friend requests, the friends you accept, and pets you care for together.

Meal photos

When you photograph a meal (a Vitto Plus feature), the photo is sent to our AI provider to estimate what's on the plate. It is not saved by us: it isn't uploaded to storage or kept in our database. We keep only the result, such as the foods, the estimated nutrition and the grade.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, and only with your permission, Vitto reads the data you allow, which can include steps, workouts, nutrition and sleep. It's used to log those activities for you and update your pet. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone except as needed to provide Vitto (see "Who we share it with"). You can change or revoke access at any time in the iPhone Settings app, under Health.

Collected automatically

  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a push token for your device, its time zone, and your quiet hours, so your pet only messages you at sensible times.
  • Usage limits: a short-lived record of how many AI requests your account makes, so we can apply daily limits. These records are deleted after about two days.

Kept only on your phone

Some features work entirely on your device and nothing is sent to us:

  • Gym location: if you save your gym, your phone checks whether you're there. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Motion: to notice when you're walking, your phone looks at the last few seconds of motion data. Nothing is stored.
  • Your reminders, appearance settings and notification preferences.

Purchases

Vitto Plus is bought through Apple's App Store and processed by Apple and our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We never see your payment card details. We receive only what's needed to unlock Plus, such as which plan you have and when it renews or ends.

How we use it

  • To run the app: saving what you log, updating your pet, and syncing across your devices.
  • To let your pet chat with you and react to what you do.
  • To estimate the nutrition in meal photos and food searches.
  • To send notifications you've allowed, such as messages from your pet, affirmations and reminders.
  • To provide Vitto Plus and check your subscription.
  • To keep Vitto secure and prevent abuse, for example with daily usage limits.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't build profiles of you for anyone else.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or others.

How long we keep it

  • Your account information, logs, pet and chats are kept until you delete your account.
  • Meal photos are never kept.
  • Usage-limit records are deleted after about two days.
  • Push tokens are removed when you sign out. Turning notifications off stops them being used.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it. If you share a pet with someone, it stays with them.
  • Notifications: turn them on or off in Settings → Notifications, or in your phone's settings.
  • Apple Health: change or revoke access in the iPhone Settings app, under Health.
  • Access or correct your information: most of it can be edited in the app. For a copy of your data, or anything else, contact us at the address below.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights over your information, such as the right to access, correct, delete or move it, or to object to how it's used. Contact us to exercise any of them.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is restricted, so each person can only read their own information, and the keys to our AI and payment providers stay on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and keep only what Vitto needs.

Children

Vitto is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Where your data is stored

Our providers may store and process your information in the United States and other countries. Wherever it's processed, it's protected as this policy describes.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
