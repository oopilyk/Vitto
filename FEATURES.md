# What Vitto can do

Vitto is a health app with a virtual pet at its centre. Everything you do for yourself (eating, training, walking, sleeping, drinking water, giving your mind a workout) looks after your pet too. Look after yourself and your pet thrives, levels up and evolves. Drift away and it gets hungry, sluggish and lonely, but it never dies and never guilts you.

Most of Vitto is free. **Vitto Plus** adds the AI-powered extras; see [Vitto Plus](#vitto-plus).

---

## The big ones

1. **A pet that lives your health.** Your meals, workouts, steps, sleep, water and mind games are what feed it, energise it and cheer it up. Its stats, moods and needs move with your real habits. See [Your pet](#your-pet).
2. **Snap a photo, get the macros (Plus).** Point the camera at your plate. Vitto names the food, estimates calories, protein, carbs and fat, grades the meal A to D, and your pet reacts in its own voice. See [Food](#food).
3. **A pet you can talk to.** Chat with it, hold the mic and speak to it, and hear it talk back out loud (Plus). It has a personality you choose or write yourself (Plus), remembers what you tell it (Plus), and messages you first when something's worth saying. See [Chat with your pet](#chat-with-your-pet).
4. **Every log is one tap from the pet's world.** Each room is a page: the Kitchen for food and water, the Gym for workouts, Outside for walks and runs, the Study for mind games. The pet is right there in a window at the top. See [The pet's world](#the-pets-world).
5. **A real workout logger.** About 137 exercises, routines, a training split that runs on the calendar, a rest timer that also shows in the Dynamic Island, personal records and strength ranks. See [Workouts](#workouts).
6. **Evolution.** From level 15, what you do most shapes who your pet becomes: Lifter, Runner or Scholar. See [Evolution](#evolution).
7. **Your pet on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.** Widgets show how it's doing and what's left today. See [Widgets and the Dynamic Island](#widgets-and-the-dynamic-island).
8. **Raise a pet together.** Share one pet with a care partner, and visit your friends' pets. See [Friends and shared pets](#friends-and-shared-pets).

## Getting started

- **Sign up** with an email and password. Vitto emails you a code to confirm the address; iPhone offers it from Mail automatically.
- **Age check.** Vitto is for people 13 and older. The birthday is used only for the check and is never stored.
- **Onboarding** asks one thing per screen, and your new pet talks you through it:
  1. **Your pet:** choose an animal, meet it and name it.
  2. **You:** your name and a username (that's how friends find you).
  3. **What your pet cares about:** pick which parts of life count (food, workouts, steps, mind games). You can change this any time.
  4. **Your body and goals:** age, sex, height, weight, whether you want to lose, keep or gain weight (with an optional target), how active you are, and how often and how you train.
  5. **Your step goal.**
  6. **What motivates you:** progress, streaks, goals, your pet, habits, friends, competition.
  7. **Your plan:** daily calories, protein, steps and training days, worked out for you.
  8. **Optional:** try Plus, pick a personality (Plus), turn on notifications, and set a first goal of 3, 7, 14 or 30 days.

## Your pet

### Ten animals
Bichon, Shiba, Otter, Tabby Cat, Bunny, Fox, Koala, Bear, Axolotl and Dino. Your first choice is free; more can be bought in the [Shop](#coins-and-the-shop).

### Levels and XP
- Everything you log earns XP.
- Levels 1 to 10 take 100 XP each. After that each level takes 15 more than the last, up to 400 XP a level from level 29.
- Every level-up pays 50 coins.

### Stats
Each stat has an explanation one tap away on the Stats screen.

| Stat | What raises it | What lowers it |
|---|---|---|
| **Health** | Balanced meals; keeping hunger, vitality and happiness comfortable | Junk food; letting a need run empty |
| **Vitality** | Workouts and walks | Slowly, each day you don't move |
| **Energy** | Sleep (most) and meals | Slowly, each day |
| **Happiness** | Every care moment, treats most of all | Slowly, each day |
| **Hunger** | Calories eaten in the last 24 hours, against what your body burns in a day | Each meal wears off a day after you ate it |
| **Mind** | Any mind game (restores it fully); a screen-time day under budget | Slowly, each day |
| **Strength** (plus Pushing, Pulling, Legs) | Lifting more than your own recent average | Never goes down |
| **Endurance** | Cardio, and days of 8,000+ steps | Never goes down |
| **Recovery** | Mobility work, sleep, sharp mind sessions | Never goes down |

- **Meals feed hunger by amount, health by quality.** A junk meal still fills the pet up, but it costs health. A balanced plate (grade A) helps both.
- **Water:** reaching the day's water goal earns XP.
- **Long breaks:** after a long time away, decay only counts the first 14 days, so coming back is never hopeless.

### Moods and how it feels
- **Moods:** happy, doing fine, hungry, sleepy (low energy) and sluggish (low vitality).
- **When something's wrong,** the pet says so and asks for the fix:
  - starving: "I'm so hungry. Feed me?"
  - exhausted: needs a walk or a workout
  - lonely
  - foggy: "Mind Gym?"
  - fading, when health runs low
- **No dying.** The worst it gets is fading.
- **Bond:** your pet's feelings toward you (devoted, warm, wary, sulking), built from how often you've shown up over the last two weeks. Two weeks of care always wins it back.
- **Tapping** the pet itself makes it hop and react with an emoji of how it feels; it gets livelier the more you tap.

### Evolution
From level 15, whatever you do most shapes who your pet becomes:
- **Lifter:** from strength training.
- **Runner:** from cardio and walking.
- **Scholar:** from mind games.

An evolution is kept for good. Earn all three and you can choose which form your pet wears, and the Stats screen shows your progress toward each form. The bear and the bunny have fully animated evolved forms: cheering, running, flexing, getting dizzy, crying and collapsing. The bichon and shiba have their own evolved art too. The other animals keep their usual look for now.

### Pet journal
At the top of each weekly recap, your pet writes a few lines in its own voice: how it's doing, what it made of your week, and what it thinks of you.

## The pet's world

Five rooms, switched with the floating bar at the bottom of the screen (Outside, Food, Home, Gym, Mind), with a light haptic tap on iPhone.

| Room | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Home** (living room) | Hang out; your trophies sit on the shelf |
| **Food** (kitchen) | Log meals and water |
| **Gym** | Log workouts |
| **Outside** | Log walks, runs and rides |
| **Mind** (study) | Play mind games |

- **Home** fills the screen. Along the top: your level; what your pet is saying; the day count, its evolution, bond and streak; how recent meals made it feel (Spicy, Cozy, Junk, and so on); and buttons for **Today** and **Chat**. A mic button lets you talk to your pet.
- **The other rooms are pages:** the room is a framed window at the top with your pet standing in it, and the logging is underneath. The window scrolls away with the page, so there's room to work.
- **Night:** the rooms switch to night versions in the evening.
- **Backdrops:** nine room styles: Classic (free), Space, Cloud, Elf, Reef, Mountain, Candy, Desert and Cyber, each with its own day and night art. Bought in the [Shop](#coins-and-the-shop).

## Logging

### Food
The Food page opens on search, with your recent meals underneath. Five tabs along its foot: **Search, Photo, Scan, Meals, Water.**

- **Photo (Plus):**
  - snap your plate, and Vitto identifies the food, estimates calories, protein, carbs and fat, and grades the meal A to D;
  - your pet reacts to it in its own voice;
  - **"Not quite right? Fix it":** tell it what the meal really was and it re-reads it (one fix a day is free, after that a fix counts as a photo);
  - up to 5 photos a day. Photos are never stored.
- **Search (free):**
  - results appear as you type, from USDA, Open Food Facts and FatSecret (branded and restaurant foods);
  - **build a plate:** tick several foods across searches (chicken, then rice, then potatoes) and log them as one meal;
  - set each food's amount in grams, ounces or servings, with − and + buttons;
  - **keep a plate in Meals** to log it again in one tap.
- **Barcode scan (free):** scan a packaged food.
- **Meals:** your saved meals (up to 30), one tap to log.
- **Recent meals:** log any recent meal again, with its amount. What you ate around this time yesterday comes first.
- **Copy yesterday's breakfast** (or lunch, dinner, snack) in one tap while today's is still empty.
- **Quick add:** for food no database has, just the calories (and protein, carbs and fat if you know them).
- **Meal slot:** breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack is set from the time of day.
- **Apple Health (free):** meals logged in apps like MyFitnessPal come in automatically.
- **Editing:** change a logged meal's name, slot or numbers later.
- **Food effects:** meals can give your pet a short-lived mood: Spicy, Wired (caffeine), Sugar rush, Cozy, Stuffed, Junk, Protein, Greens, Fresh, Sharp, Fuelled. Each comes with its own small effect and line.

### Water
- **Goal:** 2,000 mL a day (8 cups), shown in fl oz if you use imperial units.
- **Logging:** the quick-add buttons on the Food room's Water tab.
- **Reward:** the cup that reaches the goal earns your pet XP.

### Workouts
- **Exercises:** a library of about 137, by muscle group, logged as sets of weight × reps. Last time's numbers sit in a "Last" column, one tap to copy.
- **Cardio:** running, walking, cycling, rowing, elliptical, stair climber, swimming and hiking, logged as distance and minutes.
- **Timing:** the workout is timed automatically, so there's nothing to fill in.
- **Routines:** save routines (Push, Pull, Legs…). Starting one fills in its numbers, and finishing updates them.
- **Training split:** put routines and rest days in a loop of up to 14 days. It runs on the calendar, so the Gym page always shows today's session ("Today · Day 2 of 5") with a Start button. You can step it forward or back, or restart it.
- **Resume:** a workout left half done is waiting where you left it.
- **Rest timer:** starts after a set (90 seconds by default, ±15, or skip), and counts down in the Dynamic Island too.
- **Personal records:** a banner the moment you set one, plus your best bench, squat, deadlift and lat pulldown, your fastest mile or km and longest run.
- **History:** every exercise has a history one tap away.
- **Strength ranks** from Bronze to Grand Champion on the main lifts, adjusted for your bodyweight, sex and age.
- **Strength map:** a body map of which muscles you've trained.
- **Lift progress:** sessions for each lift and your distance to the next rank. The progress graph is Plus.
- **Apple Health:** workouts from other apps import automatically.

### Outside
- **One tap** to log a walk, run or ride, using your usual minutes; distance is optional, in km or miles.
- **Log again:** past activities each have a + to repeat them.
- **Outdoor routines:** save one ("Morning walk, 30 min") to log it in a tap.
- **Sync steps** on demand.

### Steps
- **Automatic:** steps come in from Apple Health on their own: when you open Vitto, whenever you come back to it, and every few minutes while it's open.
- **Big days:** 8,000+ steps counts as a big outing.
- **Goal:** your step goal can be changed on the Today screen.

### Sleep
Read from Apple Health. A good night is the biggest boost to your pet's energy, and a bad night is never punished.

### Weight
- **Weekly weigh-in:** a week after your last one, a small prompt appears on Home. Type your weight and see the change since last time, or choose "Remind me tomorrow".
- **Weight screen:** a chart with a smoothed trend line, and every weigh-in.
- **Private:** your weight never affects your pet, and friends and partners never see it.

### Screen time
Log your daily total. Lighter days reward your pet's mind; heavy days just earn less. Nothing is ever taken away. You can set a daily budget for a bonus when you come in under it. Only the total is kept, never which apps.

### Mind games (all free)
1. **Four Corners:** quick trivia; your pet runs to your answer.
2. **Pet Jeopardy:** pick squares, then wager on a final question.
3. **Daily word puzzle:** one five-letter word a day, with its own streak.
4. **Word garden:** grow words from a letter.
5. **Guess the country:** find three mystery countries by distance clues.
6. **Quick maths:** a fast arithmetic run.
7. **Read and recall:** read a passage, then answer from memory.

Every session fully restores your pet's mind.

### Undo and offline
- **Undo:** removing a log shows an Undo button. A removed log drops out of every total, chart and record.
- **Offline:** logs made without a connection count for your pet straight away and are saved to the server once you're back online. An unfinished workout is kept on the phone.

## Seeing your progress

- **Today:**
  - your level progress;
  - a card for each part of life you track: workouts, steps against your goal (with calories burned), mind games and the daily word, food (calories against target, with protein, carbs and fat), and water;
  - today's activity.
- **Trends:**
  - **Food:** over a week or a month, calories per day against your target, average macros, how often you hit your targets, plate grades and the meals you eat most;
  - **Steps:** your steps over time.
- **Weekly recap:** your week against the last one, with your pet's journal entry.
- **Insights (Plus):** your pet spots patterns in your habits, like how your sleep affects your mind games, or how protein affects the next day's workout.
- **Stats:**
  - every stat, with explanations;
  - your evolution progress;
  - your day streak and longest run;
  - a calendar of active days;
  - a breakdown of what you've logged.
- **Profile:**
  - your streak and totals;
  - calories eaten, burned and remaining today;
  - personal records and the strength map;
  - achievements;
  - shortcuts to screen time, reminders, Apple Health and your full activity history.

## Chat with your pet

- **Talking to it:** your pet chats in its own personality, knows how you've been doing, and has its own moods.
- **Speak to it:** hold the mic on Home and talk; what you say is sent as a message.
- **It talks out loud (Plus):** your pet says its lines aloud in one of 8 voices (Girl, Boy, Deep, two squeaky, two fast, and Santa).
- **It talks first sometimes:** it checks in after you log something, when you've been away, when a streak is at risk, when you usually train and haven't yet, or about something important you told it about.
- **Personalities (Plus):**
  - **Ready-made:** Feisty, Cute, Sweet, Savage, Hype, and Menace (16+).
  - **Your own (16+):** write the character in your own words.
  - **Dials:** fine-tune any of them: serious↔playful, gentle↔blunt, calm↔energetic, wholesome↔sarcastic, independent↔clingy.
- **Memory (Plus):** it remembers what you tell it (your goals, routines, upcoming events) and brings it up later. You can see and delete what it remembers in Settings. It never keeps passwords, card numbers, addresses or ID numbers.
- **Safe by design:**
  - no diet or medical advice;
  - never negative about your body, weight or food;
  - never pushes you to eat less;
  - no guilt trips.
  - If you seem to be in real distress, it drops the act and points you to real help.
- **Limits:** 5 messages a day free, 50 with Plus.

## Friends and shared pets

- **Friends:**
  - add people by username, and accept or decline requests;
  - visit a friend's pet and see how it's doing and what it's up to.
  - Friends never see what you ate, your workouts, your weight or your screen time.
- **Care partners:**
  - **Invite:** share an invite code (valid 7 days) to raise a pet together with one other person.
  - **What's shared:** you share its XP, coins, stats and room style, and each see that the other looked after it, not the details. Updates show up live.
  - **Switching:** you can have your own pet plus one shared pet, and switch between them from the top of the screen.
  - **Leaving:** you can leave any time.

## Widgets and the Dynamic Island

- **Home Screen widget, "Your pet"** (small, medium and large), light or dark with the phone:
  - **Small:** your pet with its level and mood, and calories left.
  - **Medium:** adds a calorie ring, protein, carbs and fat bars, and steps and water.
  - **Large:** adds all four macros (with fiber), steps and water bars, and today's goals.
- **Lock Screen widget, "Today":** calories and steps left, and whether a workout is due.
- **Dynamic Island and Lock Screen (iPhone, off by default):** your pet with its Food, Energy and Joy bars draining in real time and a word for how it feels ("happy", "hungry"). During a workout it shows your rest timer. Turn it on in Settings → Notifications → Dynamic Island.

## Notifications

- **Messages from your pet:** short pushes in its own voice, for example when you've been away or a streak is at risk. Never sent between 10pm and 8am.
- **Little affirmations:** a kind word every couple of days.
- **Reminders:** up to 8 of your own ("take creatine at 8am"), on the days you choose.

---

## Achievements and celebrations

- **Streaks:** any day with a workout, a meal, a mind game or a step sync of 1,000+ counts. The 🔥 on screen warns you when today's not logged yet.
- **Streak freezes:** every 7 days in a row earns a freeze (you can hold 2). A missed day uses one instead of ending your streak.
- **12 badges:** from your first meal to 250 care moments, a one-month streak and your first evolution.
- **4 golden trophies** for the living-room shelf:
  - Golden dumbbell: four weeks of hitting your weekly workout target.
  - Golden shoe: 10,000 steps a day for 30 days.
  - Golden drumstick: on target for calories and protein for 30 days.
  - Golden book: a mind game every day for 30 days.
- **Celebrations:** full-screen moments for level-ups, streak days and new achievements.
- **Share card:** a picture of your pet and what it's saying, ready to send or save.

## Settings and privacy

- **Your companion:** the Shop (animals and backdrops), what affects your pet, its personality (Plus), its voice (Plus), and what it remembers.
- **You:** update your body details, goal and pace (capped at a safe rate), activity and training, an optional custom calorie target, and whether to build muscle too; switch units between metric and imperial.
- **Notifications:** pet messages, affirmations, reminders, Dynamic Island.
- **Appearance:** System, Light or Dark.
- **Music:** optional lo-fi background music.
- **Legal:** Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and credits.
- **Delete account:** permanently removes your account, pet and history. A shared pet stays with your partner.

**What Vitto keeps private:**
- meal photos are never stored;
- screen time is kept as a total only;
- friends and partners never see your details or your weight;
- your birthday isn't stored;
- Apple Health is only ever read, never written to.

## Works with

- **Apple Health** (iPhone): reads steps, active calories, workouts, meals and sleep, so apps like Strong or MyFitnessPal can feed your pet automatically. It looks back over the last two days each time it syncs.
- **USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts and FatSecret** for food search and barcodes.
