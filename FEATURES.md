# What Vitto can do

Vitto is a health app with a virtual pet at its centre. Everything you do for yourself (eating, training, walking, sleeping, giving your mind a workout) looks after your pet too. Look after yourself and your pet thrives, levels up and evolves. Drift away and it gets hungry, sluggish and lonely, but it never dies and never guilts you.

Most of Vitto is free. **Vitto Plus** adds the AI-powered extras; see [Vitto Plus](#vitto-plus).

---

## Getting started

- **Sign up** with an email and password. Vitto emails you a code to confirm the address; iPhone offers it from Mail automatically.
- **Age check.** Vitto is for people 13 and older. The birthday is used only for the check and is never stored.
- **Onboarding** asks one thing per screen, and your new pet talks you through it:
  1. **Your pet:** choose an animal, meet it and name it.
  2. **You:** your name and a username (that's how friends find you).
  3. **What your pet cares about:** pick which parts of life count (food, workouts, steps, mind games). You can change this any time.
  4. **Your body and goals:** age, sex, height, weight, whether you want to lose, keep or gain weight (with an optional target), how active you are, and how often and how you train.
  5. **Your step goal.**
  6. **What motivates you:** progress, streaks, goals, your pet, habits, friends, competition.
  7. **Your plan:** daily calories, protein, steps and training days, worked out for you.
  8. **Optional:** try Plus, pick a personality (Plus), turn on notifications, and set a first goal of 3, 7, 14 or 30 days.

## Your pet

### Ten animals
Bichon, Shiba, Otter, Tabby Cat, Bunny, Fox, Koala, Bear, Axolotl and Dino. Your first choice is free. You can switch later for 500 coins.

### Levels, XP and coins
- Everything you log earns XP. Each level takes 100 XP.
- Every level-up pays 50 coins. Coins are spent on switching animals.

### Stats
Each stat has an explanation one tap away on the Stats screen.

| Stat | What raises it | What lowers it |
|---|---|---|
| **Health** | Balanced meals; keeping hunger, vitality and happiness comfortable | Junk food; letting a need run empty |
| **Vitality** | Workouts and walks | Slowly, each day you don't move |
| **Energy** | Sleep (most) and meals | Slowly, each day |
| **Happiness** | Every care moment, treats most of all | Slowly, each day |
| **Hunger** | Calories eaten in the last 24 hours, against what your body burns in a day | Each meal wears off a day after you ate it |
| **Mind** | Any mind game (restores it fully); a screen-time day under budget | Slowly, each day |
| **Strength** (plus Pushing, Pulling, Legs) | Lifting more than your own recent average | Never goes down |
| **Endurance** | Cardio, and days of 8,000+ steps | Never goes down |
| **Recovery** | Mobility work, sleep, sharp mind sessions | Never goes down |

- **Meals feed hunger by amount, health by quality.** A junk meal still fills the pet up, but it costs health. A balanced plate (grade A) helps both.
- **Long breaks:** after a long time away, decay only counts the first 14 days, so coming back is never hopeless.

### Moods and how it feels
- **Moods:** happy, doing fine, hungry, sleepy (low energy) and sluggish (low vitality).
- **When something's wrong,** the pet says so and asks for the fix:
  - starving: "I'm so hungry. Feed me?"
  - exhausted: needs a walk or a workout
  - lonely
  - foggy: "Mind Gym?"
  - fading, when health runs low
- **No dying.** The worst it gets is fading.
- **Bond:** your pet's feelings toward you (devoted, warm, wary, sulking), built from how often you've shown up over the last two weeks. Two weeks of care always wins it back.
- **Tapping** the pet makes it react, and it gets livelier the more you tap.

### Evolution
From level 15, whatever you do most shapes who your pet becomes:
- **Lifter:** from strength training.
- **Runner:** from cardio and walking.
- **Scholar:** from mind games.

An evolution is kept for good. Earn all three and you can choose which form your pet wears, and the Stats screen shows your progress toward each form. The bear and the bunny have fully animated evolved forms: cheering, running, flexing, getting dizzy, crying and collapsing. The bichon and shiba have their own evolved art too. The other animals keep their usual look for now.

## The pet's world

Five rooms, switched with the bar at the bottom of the screen (with a light haptic tap on iPhone):

| Room | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Living room** | Hang out; your trophies sit on the shelf |
| **Kitchen** | Log a meal |
| **Gym** | Log a workout |
| **Outdoors** | Log a run, walk, ride or swim |
| **Study** | Play mind games |

The rooms switch to night versions in the evening.

Along the top of the screen:
- your level;
- what your pet is saying;
- the day count, its evolution, bond and streak;
- how recent meals made it feel (Spicy, Cozy, Junk, and so on);
- buttons for **Today** and **Chat**.

## Logging

### Meals
- **Photo (Plus):** snap your plate. Vitto identifies the food, estimates calories, protein, carbs and fat, grades the meal A to D, and your pet reacts to it in its own voice. Photos are never stored.
- **Search (free):** look the food up in the USDA and Open Food Facts databases, with portion sizes.
- **Barcode scan (free):** scan a packaged food.
- **Apple Health (free):** meals logged in apps like MyFitnessPal come in automatically.
- **Food effects:** meals can give your pet a short-lived mood: Spicy, Wired (caffeine), Sugar rush, Cozy, Stuffed, Junk, Protein, Greens, Fresh, Sharp, Fuelled. Each comes with its own small effect and line.

### Workouts
- **Exercises:** a library of about 70, by muscle group, logged as sets of weight × reps. Adding a set copies the one before it.
- **Runs, rides and swims:** logged as distance and minutes. "Log a run" in the Outdoors room goes straight to them.
- **Timing:** the workout is timed automatically, so there's nothing to fill in.
- **Routines:** save routines (Push, Pull, Legs…). Loading one fills in last time's numbers, and finishing updates them.
- **Apple Health:** workouts from other apps import automatically.
- **Personal records:** best bench, squat, deadlift and lat pulldown, plus your fastest mile or km and longest run.
- **Strength ranks** from Bronze to Grand Champion on the main lifts, adjusted for your bodyweight, sex and age.
- **Strength map:** a body map of which muscles you've trained.
- **Lift progress:** sessions for each lift and your distance to the next rank. The progress graph is Plus.

### Steps
- **Automatic:** steps come in from Apple Health on their own: when you open Vitto, whenever you come back to it, and every few minutes while it's open.
- **Big days:** 8,000+ steps counts as a big outing.
- **Goal:** your step goal can be changed on the Today screen.

### Sleep
Read from Apple Health. A good night is the biggest boost to your pet's energy, and a bad night is never punished.

### Screen time
Log your daily total. Lighter days reward your pet's mind; heavy days just earn less. Nothing is ever taken away. You can set a daily budget for a bonus when you come in under it. Only the total is kept, never which apps.

### Mind games (all free)
1. **Four Corners:** quick trivia; your pet runs to your answer.
2. **Pet Jeopardy:** pick squares, then wager on a final question.
3. **Daily word puzzle:** one five-letter word a day, with its own streak.
4. **Word garden:** grow words from a letter.
5. **Guess the country:** find three mystery countries by distance clues.
6. **Quick maths:** a fast arithmetic run.
7. **Read and recall:** read a passage, then answer from memory.

Every session fully restores your pet's mind.

## Seeing your progress

- **Today:**
  - your level progress;
  - a card for each part of life you track: workouts, steps against your goal (with calories burned), mind games and the daily word, food (calories against target, with protein, carbs and fat);
  - today's activity.
- **Insights (Plus):** your pet spots patterns in your habits, like how your sleep affects your mind games, or how protein affects the next day's workout.
- **Stats:**
  - every stat, with explanations;
  - your evolution progress;
  - your day streak and longest run;
  - a calendar of active days;
  - a breakdown of what you've logged.
- **Profile:**
  - your streak and totals;
  - calories eaten, burned and remaining today;
  - personal records and the strength map;
  - achievements;
  - shortcuts to screen time, reminders, Apple Health and your full activity history.

## Chat with your pet

- **Talking to it:** your pet chats in its own personality, knows how you've been doing, and has its own moods.
- **It talks first sometimes:** it checks in after you log something, when you've been away, when a streak is at risk, or ahead of something important you told it about.
- **Personalities (Plus):**
  - **Ready-made:** Feisty, Cute, Sweet, Savage, Hype, and Menace (16+).
  - **Your own (16+):** write the character in your own words.
  - **Dials:** fine-tune any of them: serious↔playful, gentle↔blunt, calm↔energetic, wholesome↔sarcastic, independent↔clingy.
- **Memory (Plus):** it remembers what you tell it (your goals, routines, upcoming events) and brings it up later. It never keeps passwords, card numbers, addresses or ID numbers.
- **Safe by design:**
  - no diet or medical advice;
  - never negative about your body, weight or food;
  - never pushes you to eat less;
  - no guilt trips.
  - If you seem to be in real distress, it drops the act and points you to real help.
- **Limits:** 5 messages a day free, many more with Plus.

## Friends and shared pets

- **Friends:**
  - add people by username, and accept or decline requests;
  - visit a friend's pet and see how it's doing and what it's up to.
  - Friends never see what you ate, your workouts or your screen time.
- **Care partners:**
  - **Invite:** share an invite code (valid 7 days) to raise a pet together with one other person.
  - **What's shared:** you share its XP, coins and stats, and each see that the other looked after it, not the details.
  - **Switching:** you can have your own pet plus one shared pet, and switch between them from the top of the screen.
  - **Leaving:** you can leave any time.

---

## Notifications

- **Messages from your pet:** short pushes in its own voice, for example when you've been away or a streak is at risk. Never sent between 10pm and 8am.
- **Little affirmations:** a kind word every couple of days.
- **Reminders:** up to 8 of your own ("take creatine at 8am"), on the days you choose.
- **Dynamic Island and Lock Screen (iPhone):** your pet's needs draining in real time, with a countdown to when it next needs you.

---

## Achievements and celebrations

- **Streaks:** any day with a workout, a meal, a mind game or a step sync of 1,000+ counts. The 🔥 on screen warns you when today's not logged yet.
- **12 badges:** from your first meal to 250 care moments, a one-month streak and your first evolution.
- **4 golden trophies** for the living-room shelf:
  - Golden dumbbell: four weeks of hitting your weekly workout target.
  - Golden shoe: 10,000 steps a day for 30 days.
  - Golden drumstick: on target for calories and protein for 30 days.
  - Golden book: a mind game every day for 30 days.
- **Celebrations:** full-screen moments for level-ups, streak days and new achievements.
- **Share card:** a picture of your pet and what it's saying, ready to send or save.

## Settings and privacy

- **Your companion:** switch animals, choose what affects your pet, change its personality (Plus).
- **You:** update your body details, goal and pace (capped at a safe rate), activity and training; switch units between metric and imperial.
- **Notifications:** pet messages, affirmations, reminders, Dynamic Island.
- **Appearance:** System, Light or Dark.
- **Legal:** Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
- **Delete account:** permanently removes your account, pet and history. A shared pet stays with your partner.

**What Vitto keeps private:**
- meal photos are never stored;
- screen time is kept as a total only;
- friends and partners never see your details;
- your birthday isn't stored;
- Apple Health is only ever read, never written to.

## Works with

- **Apple Health** (iPhone): reads steps, active calories, workouts, meals and sleep, so apps like Strong or MyFitnessPal can feed your pet automatically. It looks back over the last two days each time it syncs.
- **USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts** for food search and barcodes.
